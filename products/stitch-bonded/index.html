Stitch Bonded Non

Crimp Fabrics (NCFs)

Kush manufactures Uni-directional, biaxial and multiaxial fabrics through stitch-bonding of material made of unbent fibres with no elongation. Therefore these fabrics are also called Non – Crimp Fabrics or NCFs.

Non-Crimp Fabrics offer superior mechanical performance compared to traditional woven materials because the reinforcing fibres remain straight and flat, avoiding crimp-induced stress concentrations. Stitch-bonded fabrics provide enhanced resin flow, higher tensile strength, and exceptional fatigue resistance for advanced composites in wind energy, marine, aerospace, and industrial applications.

This stitch-bonded fabric can be made using various fiber orientations with varying weights between 200 to 2000 g/m² and can be stitched with a layer of Chop Strand Mat (CSM). The maximum width can be adjusted per specific customer engineering requirements.
