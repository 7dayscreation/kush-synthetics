Furthermore, range of Kush's fabrics are approved by Germanischer Lloyd.



TESTING AT RAW MATERIAL STAGE


10 Samples are randomly drawn from the raw materials received, for 2 different tests

1. Testing of Te –Tolerance level for fibreglass with Tex 2400, 1200, 600 is set at ± 8%, and for 300 Tex roving is ± 6%
2. Loss On Ignition (LOI) –LOI should always be less then 2%.

TEST ON FINISHED FABRICS


At least one finished fabric sample is randomly drawn from the production of |each and every machine for the following tests

1. Total fabric GSM Actual weight, of a finished sample, should be within ± 5% of the intended weight of the fabric
2. Layer wise GSM Actual weight of a layer of a finished sample should be within ± 5% of the intended weigh on the fabric
3. Stitch Density Samples of finished goods are checked for density of stitches per Sq. Inch. The density varies according to fabric requirements
4. Moisture Content Every sample drawn is checked for moisture content, and the moisture content should not exceed 0.5%
