Welcome to Kush Synthetics

Kush Synthetics is a privately owned manufacturer of industrial textile products. Kush started manufacturing Multiaxial and Biaxial fabrics in 2005 at Gandhidham in Gujarat, India.

OUR PRODUCTS

Kush manufactures Uni-directional, biaxial and multiaxial fabrics through stitch-bonding of material made of unbent fibres with no elongation. Therefore these fabrics are also called Non – Crimp Fabrics or NCFs.
